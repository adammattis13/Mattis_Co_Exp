Frequently Asked Questions

What size companies do you work with?

We primarily serve Fortune 500 enterprises and ambitious mid-market companies with revenues above $100M. However, we evaluate each opportunity based on potential impact and strategic fit.

What is your typical engagement model?

We offer flexible engagement models ranging from strategic assessments (4-6 weeks) to full transformation programs (6-18 months). We also provide ongoing advisory services and embedded team augmentation.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. While headquartered in Baltimore, we serve clients globally. Our distributed team and partner network enable us to deliver value regardless of geography.

How are you different from traditional consulting firms?

We're operators, not theorists. We embed with your teams, focus on implementation not just strategy, and measure success by delivered outcomes, not billable hours. Learn more on our About page.

What is Experience Architecture?

Experience Architecture (XA) is our proprietary methodology for mapping, aligning, and optimizing all touchpoints across an enterprise. It's the discipline of creating cohesive systems that deliver value. Learn more about XA.
