Our Story

Mattis & Co. is a strategy and operations firm that doesn't feel like a consulting firm. Founded by operators, we believe in building lasting systems, not temporary fixes. Our work is rooted in decades of experience across Fortune 50 enterprises, transformations in highly regulated industries, and leadership forged in combat and corporate boardrooms alike.

We exist to help organizations build resilience, adaptability, and enduring value.

The name "anti-consultancy consultancy" isn't just marketing — it's our operating philosophy. We've been on the receiving end of traditional consulting. We've seen the binders of slides, the theoretical frameworks, the recommendations that sound brilliant in the boardroom but fall apart in implementation. We decided to build something different.

Every member of our team has been an operator. We've led transformations from the inside. We've built systems that scaled. We've made the hard decisions and lived with the consequences. This experience shapes everything we do.
