Case Study

Enabling a $3B+ Divestiture Through Operational Unification

Situation

A global education consortium of 86 universities across 200+ campuses faced a contracting market and needed to transform operations to facilitate a strategic divestiture. The organization operated as a loose federation—duplicating work, moving slowly, and struggling to deliver a consistent learner experience across its network.

Approach

We led the design and implementation of a unified operating model that streamlined curriculum development, centralized common functions, and created shared infrastructure across the network. The transformation included migrating data centers to the cloud, establishing enterprise-wide governance, and redesigning operations around the optimal learner experience—from enrollment to graduation.

Outcome

Time-to-market for new degree programs dropped by 75%. The organization achieved operational consistency across five continents, significantly reduced its debt profile, and successfully positioned itself for a divestiture valued at over $3 billion. The transformation created a competitive advantage in an otherwise contracting market.
