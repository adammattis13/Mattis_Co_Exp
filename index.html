Case Study

Scaling and Unifying 86 Institutions to Enable a $3B+ Exit

Situation

A global education network of 86 institutions across 200+ campuses operated as a loose federation—duplicating work, moving slowly, and struggling to deliver consistent results. They needed to unify operations and position for a major exit in a contracting market.

Approach

We designed and implemented a unified operating model that streamlined common functions across the network, centralized key operations, migrated infrastructure to the cloud, and built the operational consistency needed to attract buyers at scale.

Outcome

Time-to-market for new programs dropped by 75%. The organization achieved operational consistency across five continents, significantly reduced its debt profile, and successfully positioned for a divestiture valued at over $3 billion.
