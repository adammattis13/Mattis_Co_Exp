Operating Model Design

We design how work gets done—and who does it. Organizational structure, shared services, vendor strategy, and employee experience for organizations ready to operate differently.

The Challenge

Strategy without an operating model is just aspiration.

Organizations know what they want to achieve. What they struggle with is how to achieve it. The operating model—how decisions get made, how work flows, how people and processes come together—is where strategy meets reality.

Most operating model problems manifest as symptoms elsewhere: slow decision-making, unclear accountability, duplicated effort, friction between teams. Leaders treat the symptoms while the root cause persists.

Our Approach

We design operating models that work in practice, not just on paper.

We've designed operating models for organizations navigating post-merger integration, insourcing critical functions, building shared services, and transforming the employee experience. Our approach is grounded in operational reality.

Operating model work requires understanding how work actually gets done today—not how the org chart says it should. We map the real flows of work and decision-making before designing the target state.
