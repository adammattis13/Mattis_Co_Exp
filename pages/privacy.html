Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 11, 2025

At Mattis & Company, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you contact us or express interest in our services, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, company name
  • Communication Content: Messages, inquiries, and other information you choose to provide
  • Professional Information: Job title, company details, business needs (when relevant to service inquiries)

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our site:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and navigation paths
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referring URLs
  • Analytics Data: Collected through Google Analytics to understand site performance and user behavior

Job Application Information

If you apply for a position with Mattis & Company through LinkedIn, your application data is handled through LinkedIn's platform in accordance with their privacy policy. We access only the information you choose to share through your application.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide information about our services
  • Communicate with you about potential advisory engagements
  • Send you relevant business communications and updates (with your consent)
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Evaluate job applications and recruit qualified candidates
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information about you:

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about site usage. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Vercel (Hosting)

Our website is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel may collect certain technical information necessary for site operation and security.

Pipedrive (CRM)

We use Pipedrive to manage client relationships and communications. Information you provide may be stored in our CRM system for business development and client service purposes.

LinkedIn

Career opportunities are managed through LinkedIn. When you apply for a position, LinkedIn's privacy policy governs how your application data is handled.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who assist in operating our website and business (e.g., analytics, hosting, CRM)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share your information

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Contact form submissions and business inquiries are typically retained for the duration of potential business relationships and for a reasonable period thereafter for record-keeping purposes.

Your Privacy Rights

All Users

You have the right to:

  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of personal information collected, used, or shared
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service and pricing regardless of privacy rights exercise

To exercise these rights, contact us at [email protected]. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.

Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Given our work with government, defense, and enterprise clients handling sensitive business data, we maintain security practices appropriate to the nature of the information we handle.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
