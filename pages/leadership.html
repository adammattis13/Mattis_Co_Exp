Founding Partner

Adam Mattis

Founding Partner

Adam Mattis is an entrepreneur, operator, and strategic advisor who has spent his career building and scaling services businesses. He is the author of Small Firm Big Impact, a guide to building boutique advisory practices into powerhouses of influence—a playbook drawn directly from his own experience.

At Accenture, Adam led the growth of a global services practice from 250 to nearly 800 consultants generating $1.4B in revenue—effectively scaling a services business within one of the world's largest firms. He also served as Director of Digital Transformation at Laureate Education, where he helped unify operations across 86 institutions and five continents to enable a $3B+ exit.

Adam has built several businesses from the ground up, including Valor Cycle Corporation. He brings the perspective of someone who has been in the founder's seat—navigating growth ceilings, operational complexity, and the decisions that define a company's trajectory. Educated at Norwich University, he is based in the Raleigh-Durham area.
