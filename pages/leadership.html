Adam Mattis

Founding Partner

Adam Mattis is a SAFe Fellow, the highest distinction awarded by Scaled Agile, recognizing exceptional mastery and thought leadership in enterprise agility. He previously served as VP of Platform Partnerships & Advisory at Scaled Agile, where he led strategic initiatives and the SAFe Fellow program.

Before Scaled Agile, Adam led digital transformation and business agility services at Accenture, growing the global practice from 250 to nearly 800 consultants generating $1.4B in revenue. He also served as Director of Digital Transformation at Laureate Education.

Adam is an entrepreneur who has built several successful businesses, including Valor Cycle Corporation. He is the author of "Small Firm Big Impact," a guide to building boutique consulting practices into powerhouses of influence. Educated at Norwich University, he is based in the Raleigh-Durham area.
