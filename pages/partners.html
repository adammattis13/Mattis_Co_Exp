SAFe Fellows & SPCTs on Staff

Our team includes SAFe Fellows and SPCTs—the highest credentials in the Scaled Agile ecosystem.

SAFe Fellows are recognized as the most distinguished contributors to the SAFe body of knowledge. There are fewer than 50 worldwide.

SPCTs (SAFe Practice Consultant Trainers) hold the highest certification available. They are authorized to train and certify SAFe Program Consultants and have demonstrated mastery through years of fieldwork and instruction.
