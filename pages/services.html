Our Services

Four pillars of strategic advisory—built for services businesses at every stage of growth, from scaling operations to executing an exit.

Advisory

Strategic guidance for business owners navigating growth, operational complexity, or market shifts.

Running a services business is uniquely demanding. You're managing people, clients, delivery, and growth simultaneously—often without a strategic sounding board who's been in your seat. Our advisory engagements give founders and leadership teams access to experienced operators who understand the specific challenges of scaling services businesses.

We don't hand you a deck and walk away. We work alongside you as a strategic partner—helping you see around corners, pressure-test decisions, and build the clarity you need to move with confidence.

  • Fractional executive advisory and board-level counsel
  • Strategic planning and business model refinement
  • Market positioning and competitive strategy
  • Leadership team alignment and decision-making frameworks
  • Growth readiness assessment

For: Founders and leadership teams who need a strategic partner, not a consultant.

M&A

Buy-side and sell-side advisory for services businesses and the firms that fund them.

Whether you're acquiring a competitor, preparing your business for sale, or evaluating deals as a funding firm, M&A in services businesses has unique dynamics. People are the product. Culture is the moat. Integration is everything.

We bring operator experience to the deal table—helping you see past the financials to the operational reality that determines whether an acquisition creates value or destroys it.

  • Buy-side and sell-side advisory
  • Due diligence with operational depth
  • Deal structuring and valuation preparation
  • Integration planning and execution
  • Portfolio company performance advisory

For: Business owners considering acquisition or exit, and funding firms evaluating deals.

Scaling

Operational infrastructure for businesses that have outgrown their current way of working.

You've found product-market fit. Revenue is growing. But the way you operated at $5M doesn't work at $20M—and it definitely won't work at $50M. Scaling a services business means building the operational backbone that lets you grow without breaking: org design, delivery models, sales infrastructure, and the systems that hold it all together.

We've scaled services businesses across staffing, consulting, managed services, and professional services. We know where the ceilings are, and we know how to break through them.

  • Organizational design and team structure
  • Process buildout and operational systems
  • Revenue operations and sales infrastructure
  • Delivery model optimization
  • Go-to-market strategy for services businesses

For: Businesses past product-market fit that need to professionalize operations to reach the next level.

Exit Strategy

Positioning your business for a successful exit—on your terms and your timeline.

An exit isn't an event. It's a 2–5 year process of positioning your business to command the valuation you've earned. That means getting your financials clean, your operations running without you, your team capable of performing through a transition, and your business attractive to the right buyers.

We've been through this process—as operators, as advisors, and as investors. We help you build the exit runway so that when the time comes, you're positioned for the best possible outcome.

  • Exit planning and timeline development
  • Financial readiness and operational cleanup
  • Valuation optimization
  • Buyer identification and targeting
  • Transition planning and founder succession

For: Founders planning a 2–5 year exit runway who want to maximize their outcome.
