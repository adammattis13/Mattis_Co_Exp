Enterprise Strategy Consultant

Remote Full-Time

The Role

We're looking for a former executive with deep experience in corporate strategy, market positioning, and growth architecture. You've sat in the C-suite or reported directly to it. You've made the hard calls—market entry, M&A, portfolio rationalization, competitive repositioning—and you've seen what happens when strategy meets reality.

As an Enterprise Strategy Consultant, you'll lead client engagements focused on strategic clarity: helping leadership teams cut through complexity, align on what matters, and build strategies that survive contact with the market.

What You've Done

  • 10+ years in strategy roles, including C-suite or direct C-suite reporting
  • Led corporate strategy, business development, or strategic planning functions
  • Track record of successful M&A, market expansion, or business model transformation
  • Experience in consulting (MBB, Big 4 strategy, or boutique) is valuable but not required
  • Industries: Technology, Healthcare, Financial Services, or Industrial sectors preferred

Compensation

$150,000 - $250,000+ depending on experience. Equity participation for the right candidate.
